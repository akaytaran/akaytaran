### 🧭 What I do

I started as a full-stack developer. Today I run a small company where **AI agents do the engineering and I own the product**. Every change ships through measured gates, so a claim only counts once a test has confirmed it.

- 🏗️ **Product builder:** I take products end-to-end, covering architecture, security, costs and launch.
- 🤖 **AI-native workflow:** I work with a structured team of AI agents (engineering, quality, finance, marketing) under one operating model.
- 📏 **Measured, not claimed:** every release passes automated gates, and every number in my docs comes from a measurement.
- 💸 **Cost-aware by design:** my open-source work runs entirely on the user's own accounts, at zero cost to anyone else.
